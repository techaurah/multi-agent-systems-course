# Solution: History and loss: what survives a chain of summaries

Dropping timeline first gives 6 correct bands at one hop, worse than 7 when decision-maker status goes first, and the curves converge to the same 3 by four hops. Timeline carries up to 30 points against the decision-maker's 20, so it is the more expensive first drop, which the points-ascending order already knew. The general rule: a relay's drop order should be the rubric read backwards, and any relay that cannot see the rubric should not be dropping anything.
