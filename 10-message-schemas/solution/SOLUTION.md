# Solution: Message schemas: the handoff format is a correctness decision

Carrying the timeline lifts correct bands from 4 to 7 of 12. What remains lost is decision-maker status, worth 20 rubric points: orders where the lead is the decision maker still score as if they were not. The next restoration is therefore the decision-maker flag, and the pattern generalizes: restore fields in rubric order, by the points each one carries, not by how easy they are to phrase. A schema, typed or prose, is a decision about which points the downstream is allowed to earn.
