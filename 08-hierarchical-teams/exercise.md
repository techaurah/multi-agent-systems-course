# Exercise: Hierarchical teams: the manager tax, measured

Shrink the flat pool to two workers and rerun the batch of all 12 orders. What happens to the flat makespan, and at what point does the hierarchy's worse makespan (11 steps) start to look competitive? What does that tell you about what hierarchy is actually for?

Work it with the lesson's code open beside you. Every number in the worked solution comes from running a variant of that code, and you should be able to reproduce each one.
