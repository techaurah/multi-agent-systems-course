# Exercise: Termination and budgets: finish lines that are not finish lines

Run the plain pipeline, with no reviewer at all, under a step budget of 3. Everything finishes. Count the escapes and state, in one sentence, why 'finished' is the most dangerous word in this lesson's vocabulary.

Work it with the lesson's code open beside you. Every number in the worked solution comes from running a variant of that code, and you should be able to reproduce each one.
