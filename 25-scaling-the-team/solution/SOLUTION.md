# Solution: Scaling the team: where the curve bends

Five workers give a makespan of 6 steps, a speedup of 3.0 times, identical to three and four workers. The deal explains it: one worker receives two orders (six steps) and four workers receive one each (three steps), so the busiest desk still sets the clock. Between three and six workers the only thing that changes is how many desks hold a single order; the makespan does not move until the pool reaches the batch size and every order gets its own worker. Deal arithmetic first, hiring second.
